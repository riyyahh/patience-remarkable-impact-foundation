WHO WE ARE

Patience Remarkable Impact Foundation is a community development and welfare organisation committed to creating meaningful and lasting change. Through practical programmes and community-driven initiatives, we work to improve access to education, healthcare, food and nutrition, community support and opportunities for vulnerable people. Our work extends across communities, families, children and other individuals who need support, with a focus on dignity, empowerment and sustainable impact.
